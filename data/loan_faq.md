# Fictional Sahay loan product FAQ

## Interest and EMI
This fictional sample loan uses an annual reducing-balance interest rate between 16.5% and 21%, depending on the synthetic profile's inflow variability. The selected rate appears on the sample offer. The EMI calculator shows principal, total interest, processing fee and total cost before taxes. A real lender quote can differ.

## Fees and charges
The fictional sample processing fee is ₹999 before taxes. No late fee, foreclosure charge or other fee is included in the sample offer. This demo does not invent a prepayment penalty. A real customer must review the lender's Key Fact Statement and agreement for every applicable charge.

## Eligibility rules
The demo uses synthetic profile data only. It checks age 21 to 60, at least six active months, existing EMI burden, income variability and the requested amount against a versioned sample rules file. The preliminary result is not a credit bureau result, real eligibility decision or loan approval.

## Documents
The mock document flow checks image size, blur, brightness, glare, likely crop, optional OCR and name similarity against the selected synthetic profile. It never verifies a real PAN or Aadhaar. The demo accepts synthetic sample documents only.

## Mandate and disbursal
The mandate step creates a fictional reference code. It does not register e-NACH, access a bank account, disburse a loan or move money.
